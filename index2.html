About this lab

This website documents four completed Digital Humanities practical assignments. The projects bring together text preparation, computational retrieval, literary reading methods, and language-focused LLM instructions. Each stage includes a brief overview and direct links to its submitted files.

Project stages

Stage 01 · NLPCompleted

Bengali Stop Words for NLP

Prepared a Python-based Bengali stop-word resource to support preprocessing for language-analysis tasks such as keyword extraction, topic modelling, and text classification.

Focus
  • Common Bengali function words and grammatical particles
  • Organized list suitable for adaptation to different corpora
  • Duplicate removal and basic validation

Stage 02 · RAGCompleted

Extracting Indian Flora and Fauna Using RAG

Developed a local retrieval-augmented generation pipeline to identify flora and fauna names in five historical and literary texts available in English editions.

Corpus
  • Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri and Ain-i-Akbari
  • Mrigapakshishastra and Vrikshayurveda
  • Gita Govinda
Method

PDF text extraction and cleaning, overlapping text chunks, sentence-transformer embeddings, FAISS retrieval, and local LLM-based extraction through Ollama, followed by source-text checks.
